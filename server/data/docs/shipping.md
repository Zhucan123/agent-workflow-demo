# Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within contiguous regions.
Express shipping delivers within 1-2 business days and is available for
orders placed before 14:00 local time.

Orders are dispatched from the nearest regional warehouse. Tracking
numbers are emailed within 24 hours of dispatch. Insurance is included
for orders above $150.

Delivery to PO boxes is not supported. Signature is required for orders
above $250. If a package is marked as delivered but not received,
customers may file a claim within 7 days.

International shipping duties and taxes are calculated at checkout and
are non-refundable for refused deliveries.
