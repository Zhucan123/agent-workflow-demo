# Refund Policy

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all subscription plans.
To request a refund, open a ticket through the customer portal within
14 days of purchase and select "Refund" as the issue type.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business
days after approval. Pro-rated refunds apply to multi-year plans based
on unused time. Annual plans paid in full receive a full refund in the
first 30 days.

No refunds are provided for usage beyond the all-you-can-eat session
hours, promotional one-time purchases, or add-on licenses that have
already been activated.

Disputed charges are subject to manual review; the review window is up
to 10 business days.
